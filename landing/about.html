About & How to Use

Everything you need to know about Lastic Productions — features, supported platforms, and how to get started.

🎯

What is Lastic Productions?

Lastic Productions is a free, open-source multi-platform video and audio downloader built with Python, Kivy, Flask, and yt-dlp. It lets you download videos and extract audio from dozens of platforms in one clean, dark-mode interface.

📥

How to Use (Web)

  1. Click Use Tool or Open Web Downloader to launch the online app.
  2. Paste your video URL into the input field.
  3. Click Analyze to fetch video info, thumbnail, and available qualities.
  4. Select your quality (Best / 1080p / 720p / 480p / 360p) or choose Audio Only for MP3.
  5. Click Download and save the file to your device.
🌐

Supported Platforms

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, Reddit, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, Pinterest, LinkedIn, and most direct video links. Playlists and bulk archives are supported on the web version.

⚙️

Tech Stack

  • UI: Kivy 2.x (mobile & desktop)
  • Web: Flask + Gunicorn
  • Engine: yt-dlp
  • Packaging: Buildozer (Android APK)
  • Media: FFmpeg (audio conversion)
📱

Versions Available

  • Web: Browser-based, no install — works on phone & PC
  • Desktop: Windows, macOS, Linux (Kivy app)
  • Mobile: Android APK (API 21+, arm64-v8a)
