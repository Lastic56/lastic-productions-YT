# 🎯 Features Breakdown

## ✅ Multi-Platform Support
- **YouTube**: Supports HD, 4K, and Playlists.
- **Social Media**: Downloads from Instagram (Posts, Reels, IGTV), Twitter/X, Facebook, TikTok.
- **Other**: Reddit, Vimeo, and most direct video links.

## ✅ Quality Control
- **Best Available**: Automatically selects the highest quality video+audio.
- **Resolution Select**: Manually choose 1080p, 720p, 480p, or 360p.
- **Audio Only**: Extracts audio and saves as high-quality MP3.

## ✅ Professional UI/UX
- **Real-time Progress**: Visual progress bar with percentage.
- **Stats**: Shows current download speed and ETA.
- **Status Log**: Scrollable history of actions and errors.
- **Dark Mode**: Easy on the eyes for night usage.

## ✅ Technical Specs
- **Threading**: Downloads run in background threads to keep UI responsive.
- **Permissions**: Auto-requests Android storage permissions on runtime.
- **Storage**: Saves directly to Android `Download` folder.
