About & How to Use

What is Lastic Productions?

A free, open-source multi-platform video downloader built with Python, Kivy, Flask, and yt-dlp.

How to Use (Web)

  1. Click Use Tool or Launch Web Downloader.
  2. Paste your video URL into the input field.
  3. Click Analyze to fetch video info.
  4. Select quality or audio format.
  5. Click Download and save your file.

Supported Platforms

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, Reddit, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, Pinterest, LinkedIn, and most direct video links.

Disclaimer

Only download content you own or have permission to save. Respect each platform's Terms of Service. Not affiliated with YouTube, X, Instagram, or TikTok.
